    Our story

    A Roodepoort practice built on integrity, care and doing things properly.

    "Our success of almost 30 years is the result of hard-working, dedicated and competent people working together as a team."

    Barbara De Jager established De Jager Attorneys Incorporated in 1997, after relocating from Polokwane, Limpopo to Roodepoort as a practising Attorney, Conveyancer and Notary Public.

    Today the firm is based on the corner of Hendrik Potgieter and Fredenharry Road in Strubens Valley. We help individuals, families and businesses with property transfers, antenuptial contracts, wills, deceased estates and notarial work.

    At the heart of our practice is a commitment to our clients. We take the time to listen, understand your situation and give practical advice that is clear and efficient.
